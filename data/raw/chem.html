Chemistry, Faculty of Science

CHEM_V: Chemistry

Students without BC Secondary School Chemistry 12 or equivalent are required to take the UBC Chemistry Basic Skills Test. Students receiving a satisfactory score on this Test take CHEM_V 111. With permission of the department head, students not receiving a satisfactory score on this Test take CHEM_V 100. Students with credit for CHEM_V 100 may take CHEM_V 121. Students with BC Secondary School Chemistry 12 or equivalent must take CHEM_V 121, CHEM_V 141 or CHEM_V 154. CHEM_V 100 and CHEM_V 111 are not open to students with credit for Chemistry 12. CHEM_V 141 is suggested for students with an academic interest in lab-focused sciences such as Chemistry, Biochemistry, or similar subjects. Students with credit for CHEM_V 111, CHEM_V 121 or CHEM_V 141 may take CHEM_V 123, a requirement for many Science programs. The following courses are for students in the Faculty of Applied Science: CHEM_V 154, CHEM_V 250, CHEM_V 251, CHEM_V 260. The Department lists courses that comprise the individual lecture and laboratory components for courses that contain both. Transfer students with 4 credits of CHEM 1st will be deemed to have satisfied the requirement of CHEM_V 121. Transfer students with 8 credits of CHEM 1st will be deemed to have satisfied both the CHEM_V 121 and CHEM_V 123 requirements.
